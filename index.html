Our Core Pillars

Monsoon-Resilient Engineering

Kerala receives over 3,000mm of annual rainfall. Every Tattva home incorporates rubble footing waterproofing, slope-graded stormwater channels, deep eaves, and slab flood ponding tests.

Passive Bioclimatic Cooling

Our Nadumuttam central courtyards act as natural thermal chimneys, drawing hot air upward and circulating cool breezes misted over lily ponds, reducing AC energy demand by up to 45%.
