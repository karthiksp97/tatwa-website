## Brand & Style

This design system translates the discipline of tropical modernism and contextual Kerala architecture into a high-utility digital experience. The interface balances the restraint of an editorial monograph with the functional precision required for cost estimations, blueprint inspections, and project milestones. 

The aesthetic is grounded in tactile minimalism—drawing from exposed laterite stone, aged teak wood, lime-plaster finishes, and monsoon verandahs. The experience avoids synthetic gloss, artificial neons, and generic SaaS patterns. Instead, it relies on disciplined spatial proportions, crisp structural grids, warm earthen materials, and quiet micro-interactions that evoke confidence, permanence, and bespoke craft for discerning homeowners.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural architectural materials native to Kerala, anchored by unbleached plaster, seasoned timber, and fired clay.

- **Primary (`#C05A3E`)**: Fired Terracotta. Used deliberately for focal calls-to-action, primary interactive states, active structural tags, and key design indicators.
- **Secondary (`#1E2229`)**: Deep Basalt Charcoal. Used for primary text, structural lines, high-contrast badges, and grounding elements.
- **Tertiary (`#9E4329`)**: Aged Teak / Burnished Clay. Used for pressed states, key architectural callouts, and secondary accents.
- **Neutral Base (`#FAF8F5`)**: Natural Lime Wash. Serves as the primary viewport background, delivering warm readability without eye fatigue.
- **Neutral Container (`#F4F0EA`)**: Calico Linen. Applied to canvas cards, elevated modules, and table headers.
- **Neutral Border & Muted Stone (`#E5DFD5`)**: Cut Granite / Sand. Used for hairline architectural dividers, inactive borders, and segmented control containers.
- **Functional Dark Surfaces (`#16191E`)**: Monolithic Charcoal. Reserved for plan viewer toolbars, technical overlays, and evening presentation modes.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a dialogue between architectural warmth and construction precision:

- **Headlines (Noto Serif)**: Brings an editorial, literary tone evocative of high-end architectural monographs. Used for primary page titles, project names, and featured spatial concepts.
- **Body & Controls (Manrope)**: A crisp, balanced geometric grotesque that maintains clarity across material specifications, client correspondence, and mobile viewports.
- **Technical Annotations & Numerical Data (JetBrains Mono)**: Conveys engineering rigor. Applied strictly to structural measurements (sq.ft, cubic meters), estimation spreadsheets, currency values (₹ INR), stage percentages, and cadastre/survey notations.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a modular, publication-style grid that emphasizes intentional white space and structured alignment.

- **Grid Architecture**:
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column system, 16px gutters, 20px side margins.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column system, 24px gutters, 40px side margins.
  - **Desktop (≥ 1200px)**: 12-column system, 24px gutters, 64px side margins with a maximum canvas width of 1408px (88rem).
- **Rhythm**: Spacing is derived from an 8pt base unit. Section boundaries breathe through substantial vertical offsets (`space-3xl` and `space-4xl`), honoring the generous proportions of courtyard-centric (Nadumuttam) Kerala homes.
- **Reflow Rules**: Complex multidimensional project matrices, BOQ (Bill of Quantities) tables, and timeline milestones collapse into vertically stacked, touch-friendly tactile cards on viewports under 768px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is articulated through material layering, low-contrast ghost borders, and ultra-soft, warm-tinted ambient shadows that simulate natural tropical daylight entering a sheltered veranda.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#FAF8F5` lime wash. Completely flat.
- **Layer 1 (Substrates & Embedded Modules)**: `#F4F0EA` linen surface framed with a 1px continuous hairline border of `#E5DFD5`. Zero shadow.
- **Layer 2 (Resting Cards & Interactive Modules)**: `#FFFFFF` or `#F4F0EA` accompanied by a 1px border of `#E5DFD5` and an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.02)`.
- **Layer 3 (Floating Sheets, Blueprint Drawers & Flyouts)**: Elevated white surfaces with a crisp `#E5DFD5` boundary and deep diffuse illumination: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.08), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.03)`.
- **Layer 4 (Modals & Lightbox Overlays)**: Framed against a semi-opaque charcoal veil: `rgba(22, 25, 30, 0.65)` with a backdrop blur of `8px`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs soft, calibrated geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Radii are kept minimal (4px base, 8px on larger containers, and 12px on modal windows) to evoke the precise joinery of traditional timber architecture, dressed granite basements (Adhisthana), and modern rectilinear concrete slabs. Overly circular pill shapes are avoided to preserve structural formality and professional architectural credibility.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: `#C05A3E` background, `#FFFFFF` text, subtle 4px radius, 0 2px 8px rgba(192, 90, 62, 0.2) shadow. Active state shifts to `#9E4329`.
  - *Secondary*: Transparent background with a 1.5px border of `#1E2229`, `#1E2229` text, transitioning on hover to `#1E2229` background with `#FAF8F5` text.
  - *Tertiary / Ghost*: Unbordered, `#1E2229` text, accompanied by an animated terracotta underline indicator.

- **Cards & Architectural Spec Blocks**:
  - Encased in 1px `#E5DFD5` borders over `#FFFFFF` or `#F4F0EA` backgrounds.
  - Generous internal padding (24px to 32px).
  - Hover states subtly tint the top border with a 2px `#C05A3E` stroke rather than lifting abruptly.

- **Input Fields & Cost Estimator Selectors**:
  - Surface: `#FFFFFF`. Border: 1.5px `#E5DFD5`. Text: `#1E2229`. Placeholder: `#8F8A81`.
  - Focus: 1.5px `#C05A3E` perimeter outline with zero heavy exterior halo.
  - Numeric entries are strictly typeset in JetBrains Mono.

- **Chips & Filter Tags**:
  - Compact 4px radius with 1px `#E5DFD5` border, `#F4F0EA` background, Manrope bold 11px uppercase typography.
  - Selected state: `#1E2229` background with `#FAF8F5` text.

- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Square edges with a minimal 2px radius for checkboxes, circular for radios. 
  - Unchecked: 1.5px `#1E2229` stroke over clear background. Checked: `#C05A3E` fill with an off-white architectural check/dot mark.

- **Lists & Phase Progress Trackers**:
  - Timeline markers emulate structural survey datum pins.
  - Row dividers use fine 1px `#E5DFD5` horizontal rules.
  - Step milestones utilize JetBrains Mono step enumerations (e.g., `01 / FOUNDATION`, `02 / SUPERSTRUCTURE`).

- **Domain-Specific Components**:
  - *Floor Plan Viewport*: Inverted dark-mode chassis (`#16191E`) with floating `#FAF8F5` scale indicators, coordinate callouts, and terracotta measurement dimensions.
  - *Material Swatch Cards*: Dual-pane cards juxtaposing physical material imagery (Laterite, Teak, Kota Stone) with carbon calculation, source location, and square-foot cost estimates.
